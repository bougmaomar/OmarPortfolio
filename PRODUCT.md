## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are small-business owners across Morocco who need a website, an
internal tool, or automation but don't speak software. They are evaluating a
developer without the vocabulary to judge one, and are wary of being overcharged,
locked in, or abandoned after launch. They are served equally in French, Arabic,
and English — no single audience leads; the site treats FR, AR, and EN as
first-class, including French/English-speaking and international or expat clients.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and lead-generation site for Omar Bougma, a software
developer in Marrakesh. It exists to turn a stranger who is unsure what they need
into a booked first conversation. Success is a qualified inquiry — an email, a
WhatsApp message, or a booked free consultation — from a small business that
trusts Omar enough to start.

## Positioning

Clarity and ownership over jargon and lock-in. Omar leads with the client's
problem, not a technology stack ("the right medium for the real problem"), and
commits out loud to the things a burned client fears most: a fixed written quote
before any work, plain language throughout, and full ownership of the code, data,
domain, and accounts. His edge is range under one person: full-stack development,
DevOps/hosting, and security handled by the same person who owns the outcome from
first idea to running production — so nothing is thrown over a wall or abandoned
at launch.

## Product Principles

1. **Lead with their world, not my skill list.** Every surface starts from the
   client's problem and fear, not the technology.
2. **Ownership is the promise.** No lock-in, no jargon, no markup, no vanishing
   after launch — the client keeps everything.
3. **Fixed and stated up front.** A written quote and clear price precede any
   work; nothing is vague or open-ended.
4. **Honesty over polish.** Never overstate proof; missing metrics and
   testimonials stay honestly absent until real ones exist.
5. **Trilingual as a first-class constraint.** FR, AR (RTL), and EN are peers;
   nothing ships that only works in one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Full right-to-left support for Arabic is a functional requirement, not an
enhancement. The audience includes non-technical readers, so plain language and
legible, high-contrast typography across all three scripts are part of the
product's job.
